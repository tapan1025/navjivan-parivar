Our Story & Mission

A Family Away From Family

Navjivan Senior Parivar is a warm, vibrant community for Indian seniors living in South Florida. We believe that life's richest chapters are filled with laughter, connection, and cultural pride — and we are here to make that happen.

Founded with love, our Parivar (family) brings together seniors from all Indian backgrounds — Gujarati, Hindi, Tamil, Punjabi, Marathi and more — to celebrate shared traditions, support one another's wellness, and create memories that last a lifetime.

Whether you are new to Florida or have lived here for decades, you will always find a warm smile and open arms in our community.

Frequently Asked Questions
❁
Navjivan Senior Parivar is a welcoming senior community group for Indian seniors in South Florida. Our goal is to bring seniors together through friendship, wellness programs, cultural events, and social gatherings. "Navjivan" means "New Life" — and that is exactly what we offer every member.
Our community is open to all Indian seniors (60+) residing in South Florida and the surrounding areas. We welcome seniors from all Indian states, languages, and backgrounds. Friends and family of members are also invited to join our public events!
Simply click the "Join Our WhatsApp Group" button on this website or ask an existing member to add you. The group is called "South Florida Senior Event Group" and is moderated to keep it respectful and welcoming for all.
We organize a wonderful variety of events including: Yoga & Meditation mornings, Bhajan Sandhya (devotional evenings), Diwali & Holi celebrations, potluck luncheons, cultural film screenings, health awareness talks, Garba nights, birthday celebrations, and much more!
Most of our gatherings and community events are free of charge. Some special events may have a small voluntary contribution to cover venue or food costs. There is no mandatory membership fee — our community is built on love, not money.
On this website, simply find the event you wish to attend, click your name in the attendees list, or click the RSVP button to add yourself. You can also RSVP directly in our WhatsApp group when events are announced there.
You can reach our Senior Director and team through the WhatsApp group or by messaging directly through the group. Our leadership team is always happy to assist with questions, event ideas, or any concerns.
